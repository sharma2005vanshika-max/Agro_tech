PestWatch Live Map
AI-powered crop disease detection, early warning, and community-based agricultural monitoring.

Overview
PestWatch Live Map is a web and mobile platform designed to help farmers detect crop diseases early and prevent their spread.
Farmers can upload geotagged crop images, which are analyzed by an AI model to identify possible diseases. Individual reports are aggregated to generate a village-level live risk map, helping farmers, FPOs, and agricultural extension officers monitor emerging disease hotspots.
When disease risk increases in an area, farmers receive simple Hindi voice alerts along with preventive recommendations based on their crop and growth stage.

Problem
Crop diseases are often detected only after they have spread significantly. Farmers may lack:
Early information about disease outbreaks
Access to agricultural experts
Localized disease-risk information
Timely preventive recommendations
Simple digital tools for reporting crop problems
This can lead to delayed treatment, crop losses, and unnecessary use of pesticides.

Our Solution
PestWatch follows a simple cycle:

Report →  Detect →  Map →  Alert →  Prevent
Farmers upload crop images.
AI analyzes the image for possible diseases.
Reports are mapped using location data.
Multiple reports are combined to identify disease hotspots.
Farmers receive localized alerts when risk increases.
FPOs and extension officers can monitor and respond to outbreaks.
